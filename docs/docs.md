## 1 **Short Description, Your one-line elevator pitch**

"NomaApp is an AI-powered crop doctor in every farmer's pocket — instantly diagnosing plant diseases and pests from a photo, so smallholder farmers stop guessing and start saving their harvests."

## 2 **Problem You Are Solving**
Describe the specific problem your venture addresses

**"NomaApp is an AI-powered crop doctor in every farmer's pocket — instantly diagnosing plant diseases and pests from a photo, so smallholder farmers stop guessing and start saving their harvests."**

## 3. Most Affected Users*
Who is most affected by this problem?

Smallholder farmers in Nigeria and Sub-Saharan Africa, people farming less than 2 hectares of land, living season to season, with no safety net. When a disease wipes out their maize or tomatoes, there's no insurance payout, no backup plan. It's a family that doesn't eat, kids pulled from school, and debt that takes years to recover from. These are over 30 million farmers feeding the world - and they're the least equipped to protect their crops.

## 4 Importance of Solving the Problem*
Why is this problem important to solve now?

Pests and diseases destroy up to 40% of global crops every year, a trillion-dollar loss that falls hardest on the farmers who can least afford it. Climate change is making it worse, pushing new pests into new regions faster than farmers can adapt. At the same time, smartphones are reaching rural communities at scale for the first time, and AI is finally good enough to diagnose a crop disease from a photo with expert-level accuracy. The window to build this is right now, before the problem gets bigger and before someone else closes it.

## 5 Existing Alternatives*
What are the existing solutions to this problem?

Most farmers guess, ask a neighbor, or wait for an extension officer who visits once a month. Apps like Plantix exist but were built for connected users with decent smartphones, not for a farmer in rural Nigeria on a 3G network. And even the best tools stop at diagnosis. None of them tell you what to buy, where to get it, or whether you can afford it. That last mile is completely unsolved.

## 6 Proposed Solution*
What is your proposed solution?

NomaApp lets farmers take a photo of a sick crop and get an instant AI diagnosis, what the disease is, how serious it is, and exactly what to do about it. It works offline, runs on low-end Android phones, and responds in local languages. Beyond diagnosis, farmers can access treatment recommendations, connect to nearby agro-dealers, and get early warnings when a disease is spreading in their area. One app that takes a farmer from "something is wrong" to "here's how to fix it" in under a minute.

## 7 Solution Differentiation*
How is your solution better than existing options?

Three things set NomaApp apart.

First, it works offline, no internet, no problem. Most competitors fail the moment a farmer steps outside a city. Second, it's built for the actual user, low-end Android phones, local languages, and crop varieties specific to African and Asian farming contexts, not Western research datasets. Third, it doesn't just diagnose - it connects farmers to treatment, pricing, and nearby suppliers in the same app. Competitors hand you a diagnosis and leave you stranded. NomaApp takes you all the way to the fix.

## 9 Target Users*

Smallholder farmers in Nigeria and Sub-Saharan Africa, specifically those farming staple crops like maize, cassava, tomatoes, and rice on less than 2 hectares of land. Most own a basic Android smartphone, operate in low-connectivity environments, and make farming decisions alone without access to professional advice. Secondary users include agricultural extension officers, farmer cooperatives, and agro-input dealers who work directly with these farmers every day.
